Welcome

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Pennsylvania, where I am advised by Val Tannen and Steve Zdancewic. My interests lie in formal verification, programming languages, and highly reliable networks. For the past two summers I have interned at NASA Langley Research Center where I am investigating software-defined, delay/distruption-tolerant, and mobile ad-hoc networking applied to emergency and disaster response scenarios. The common thread in my research is combining theory with practice to make sure systems do what they are designed to do reliably and verifiably.

I will be on the job market in 2024. I am interested in public, academic, or industrial research in networks and programming languages, particularly where this concerns real-world critical systems.
